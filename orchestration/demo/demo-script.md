# Vasooli Agent demo script

## Problem — 30 seconds

Indian MSMEs regularly wait months for payments, losing working capital and often lacking a practical way to use the MSMED Act timeline.

## Solution — 45 seconds

Vasooli is a voice-first Hindi collections agent. It captures an invoice by phone, remembers buyer commitments, checks payment, and takes timely, transparent follow-up action.

## Demo — 90 seconds

Call the intake agent and give an invoice number, buyer, amount and due date. Open the dashboard to show the new card and its legal clock. Open Aarav's card to show a previous call. Open Narmada's card at day 46, then open its complaint PDF to show the principal, statutory interest and total claim.

## Sponsor stack — 30 seconds

ElevenLabs powers natural Hindi voice intake and outbound calling. The Breeth captures buyer intent. n8n orchestrates clocks and escalation. Dodo verifies payment. Render hosts our Next.js dashboard, Express API and PostgreSQL database.

## Impact — 15 seconds

Vasooli turns a missed follow-up into a reliable, documented collection process so small businesses can protect cash flow.
